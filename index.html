01 / About

A life that happens to include engineering.

Research matters to me, but it is only one part of how I spend my time and what I am curious about.

I study mechanical engineering and enjoy problems that sit between mechanics, materials, manufacturing, sensing, and control. Outside coursework and research, I follow football, listen to a lot of music, travel whenever I can, and keep photographs of places and ordinary moments that I do not want to forget.

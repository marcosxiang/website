01 / About

Engineering with a systems view.

I like problems where mechanics, materials, manufacturing, electronics, and computation have to work together.

I am currently pursuing undergraduate training in Mechanical Engineering. My interests sit at the boundary between classical engineering fundamentals and emerging intelligent-device technologies. I am especially interested in how material selection, structural design, fabrication strategy, and signal interpretation can be combined into compact systems that sense, respond, and adapt.

Current focusSmart devices

Wearable sensing, flexible structures, multifunctional interfaces.

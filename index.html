01 / About

Curious about how things work — and how to make them better.

My work starts with engineering fundamentals and moves toward devices that have to function in the real world.

I am currently pursuing undergraduate training in Mechanical Engineering. My interests sit between classical mechanics and emerging smart-device technologies. I enjoy problems that require more than one discipline: a material that changes a signal, a structure that creates a useful response, or a control system that makes a mechanical platform behave reliably.
